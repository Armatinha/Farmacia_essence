#### Overall Impression
An exceptionally clean, premium foundation that nails the "scientific luxury" brief. The biggest opportunity is elevating the micro-interactions and replacing native browser defaults to maintain total immersion.

#### What's Working
1. **Visual Hierarchy**: The `eyebrow` -> `Title` -> `Description` pattern is flawlessly executed across all pages, guiding the user's eye perfectly.
2. **Thematic Consistency**: The alternating dark and light mode sections maintain the brand identity without causing visual fatigue.
3. **State Management**: The Authentication page's feedback states (idle, loading, success, warning, error) are clear, beautifully styled, and reassuring.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Form Submission UX**: The Contact form uses a native browser `alert()` for submission success.
  - **Why it matters**: Native alerts completely break the premium, immersive feel of the rest of the site.
  - **Fix**: Implement a custom toast notification or in-page success state that matches the visual quality of the Auth page.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Lack of Micro-interactions**: The product cards only have a hover state.
  - **Why it matters**: For a high-end brand, static pages can feel slightly stiff.
  - **Fix**: Add subtle scroll-driven reveal animations (fade and slight translate-y) as sections enter the viewport.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable animate`

- **[P3] Typography Impact**: The typography is safe and clean, but could be bolder.
  - **Why it matters**: A more aggressive scale on massive headlines would increase the "wow" factor.
  - **Fix**: Adjust letter-spacing (tighter tracking) on `text-7xl` and above for a more editorial feel.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`
